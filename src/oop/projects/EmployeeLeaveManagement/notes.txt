in leaveRequest.java i made a design mistake that was keeping the same approve and deny in all subclasses of LeaveRequest.
It could be simplified by just keeping approve and deny in parent LeaveRequest class and keep the processApplication method in
subclasses.
Reason: Because, approve and deny will call process application as a guard and approve/deny are actions taken by a person (HR)
So it will be redundant to implement these classes in all subclasses of leaveRequest.
